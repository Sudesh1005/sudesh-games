CARROMRAJA

A real carrom board in 3D. Pocket your coins, cover the queen, win the board.

How to play
  • Place the striker on your base line with the slider (or drag along the line).
  • Shoot: touch the board, pull back and let go. The further you pull, the harder the shot. The dotted line shows where it goes.
  • First player plays white coins, the other side black. In 4 players, partners sit opposite each other.
  • Pocket one of your coins and you shoot again.
  • The red queen must be covered: pocket one of your coins in the same shot or the next one, or she goes back to the centre.
  • Foul: if the striker goes in, you lose your turn and one of your coins comes back.
  • Pocket all 9 of your coins (with the queen covered) to win. Points: the other side's coins left, +3 if you covered the queen.
